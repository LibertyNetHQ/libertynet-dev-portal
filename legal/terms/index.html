LibertyNet is a prototype, offered free of charge, with no guarantees. This page says what that means concretely.

1. What this is

LibertyNet Compute lets you describe a data task, sends it to a compute provider, and returns a result with evidence you can check. The coordination API underneath it is public and anyone may call it.

It is prototype software. It is not a finished product, it carries no service level, and parts of it are explicitly unfinished — those parts are named in section 4 rather than left for you to discover.

2. No account, no signup

There is nothing to register for. Your identity is a keypair your browser generates and a DID derived from it; you prove who you are by signing each request. We issue no API keys and accept no passwords or bearer tokens.

A consequence worth stating plainly: we cannot recover anything for you. There is no "forgot my password" because there is no password. If you reload the page, your browser makes a new key and your earlier sessions become unreadable — to you and to us alike. We cannot tell you apart from a stranger who guessed your session identifier, which is precisely what makes the system private, and also what makes recovery impossible.

3. It is free, and nothing is charged

There is no charge for using LibertyNet today. We take no payment, hold no payment instrument, and have no billing system.

You will see prices quoted in "Fuel" and receipts recording amounts. These do not move value. The settlement engine is a stub: the coordinator runs with no rate card configured, fuel settlement is denied by default, and no delivery is ever charged. A receipt is an accounting record of what would have been owed, not evidence of a payment.

The wallet at libertynet.ai/wallet connects to the Ethereum Sepolia test network. Balances and tokens there carry no real value and cannot be exchanged for anything. Do not send real assets to any address shown by this software.

4. What is unfinished

Stated here rather than discovered later:

  • Settlement is a stub. See section 3.
  • Confidential compute is unavailable. TEE, remote attestation, no-egress, private runtime, FHE, zkVM and differential privacy are all shown as Pending or Unavailable in the product, and a task requesting Confidential privacy is refused rather than quietly downgraded to Standard.
  • Your identity does not persist. A page reload is a new identity. See section 2.
  • There is no staging environment. Changes are deployed straight to the system you are using.
  • Log retention is undefined. See the Privacy page.
  • Provider nodes are all ours. The architecture is for a network of independent providers; today there is not one.

5. Your data may be deleted

Coordination state is now stored durably and survives a restart, which was not true before 16 August 2026. It is still a prototype: we may delete data during maintenance, during a migration, or by accident, without notice and without a way to restore it.

Download anything you want to keep. Result bytes are held on the provider node, not by the page you are looking at.

6. What you agree not to do

  • Do not upload data you do not have the right to process, or that you would mind us holding. Files you attach are uploaded to our server and to the node that runs your task.
  • Do not upload unlawful content, malware, or anything designed to attack the runtime that executes it.
  • Do not attempt to access another user's session, result or receipt.
  • Do not attempt to overwhelm the service. The API is rate limited per identity and per source address; a 429 with a retry_after_ms is a request to slow down, and repeatedly ignoring it is abuse rather than enthusiasm.
  • Do not use the platform where its failure would cause harm — anything safety-critical, medical, financial or legally consequential. It has no service level and it is not built for that.

We may rate-limit, refuse or remove anything, at any time, without notice. There is no appeals process, because there is no account system to run one through.

7. Your content stays yours

You keep all rights in the data you upload and the results you receive. We claim no ownership and no licence beyond what running your task requires: storing your input, passing it to the provider node that executes it, and returning the output to you.

We do not use your data to train models. We do not sell it. See Privacy for what is recorded and where.
